Our story

The art of slowing down.

Founded in 2011 by Emirati entrepreneur Najla Al Ansari, Polished Spa was created as a women-only retreat for body care, nail artistry and meaningful rest.

For more than fourteen years, every detail has served one feeling: leave lighter than you came. From the first welcome to the final cup of ginger tea, this is your space to slow down and reconnect.
